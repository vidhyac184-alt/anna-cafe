Our Heritage

The story behind Anna Café

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Passion

Founded in 2020, Anna Café brings the soul-satisfying flavors of Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Karnataka straight to your table. Our master chefs use time-honored techniques, stone-ground batters, and hand-selected spices imported directly from Southern farms.

Whether it is the morning aroma of freshly brewed filter coffee or the crispy crackle of a ghee roast dosa, every bite tells a story of heritage hospitality and authentic culinary artistry.
